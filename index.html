PBK Progression Rules

White through Advanced Blue use 2 stripes per rank. Purple, Red, and Brown use 4 stripes. Advanced Purple, Advanced Red, and Advanced Brown use 2 stripes. After Advanced Brown comes Training Cycle, followed by 1st through 10th Degree Black Belt.
