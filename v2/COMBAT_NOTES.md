# PBK V2 Combat Pass — Current Direction

## Milestone focus
This pass stays locked to one polished point-sparring match.

## Player feel targets
- Responsive lateral and forward/back movement
- Fast entry without floaty acceleration
- Clear guard stance while idle
- Punch, side kick, round kick, and blitz entry should read as different techniques
- Contact only scores during active attack frames and while facing the opponent
- Missed attacks should leave a short recovery window
- Blocking should be obvious and should stop scoring
- Fighters should physically separate instead of overlapping

## Presentation targets
- PBK-branded dojo environment
- Better camera framing as fighters move apart
- Visible contact flash / hit spark
- Stronger score callout and winner state
- Touch controls must remain usable on Android

## Next quality gate
Do not expand the RPG world until the sparring slice feels credible enough to hand to Bradley as a game rather than as a systems test.
