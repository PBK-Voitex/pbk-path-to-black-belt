# PBK V2 Contact Polish QA

Milestone 1 contact pass acceptance checks:

- Punch only scores when the attacking fist reaches the opponent body/head zone during the active frame.
- Side kick and round kick only score when the attacking foot reaches the opponent body/head zone during the active frame.
- Attacks that visually miss do not score.
- Blocking prevents a point and produces a blocked hit response.
- Angle-out movement can evade an incoming active strike.
- Blitz only starts from useful point-sparring range and must still make contact to score.
- Recovery windows remain punishable by the AI.
- Fighters do not overlap through each other at close range.
- Score pauses action and resets both fighters to center.
- First to 5 ends the match cleanly.
- Camera keeps both fighters readable during movement and contact.
- Desktop and touch inputs still expose all Milestone 1 actions.
