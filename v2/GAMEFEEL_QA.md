# PBK V2 Game-Feel QA

Milestone 1 acceptance checks for the current sparring slice.

## Contact and scoring
- Punch scores 1 only during its active window and only in range/facing.
- Side kick scores 2 only during its active window and only in range/facing.
- Round kick scores 2 only during its active window and only in range/facing.
- Blitz scores 1 and requires a real entry from medium distance.
- Block prevents scoring and produces distinct feedback.
- Angle-out creates a short evade window and lateral displacement.
- A clean hit pauses/reset-centers both fighters before the next exchange.
- First fighter to 5 wins.

## Feel
- Hit confirmation includes spark, camera shake, exposure flash, and brief slow-motion.
- Missed attacks leave a recovery window that the AI can punish.
- Fighters never occupy the same space.
- Camera keeps both fighters visible during movement and scoring.
- Controls remain responsive on keyboard and coarse-pointer/mobile layouts.

## Martial-arts authenticity
- Guard pose remains up by default.
- Side kick is long-range and slower than punch.
- Round kick has distinct animation and timing.
- Blitz closes distance aggressively rather than scoring from too far away.
- Defensive angle-out is available to escape pressure instead of only backing straight up.
