# Combat Smoke Checklist

Run this checklist before calling a PBK V2 browser build player-ready.

- Page boots without fatal errors.
- Bradley and Ryan both render above the mat.
- Camera keeps both fighters in frame during separation.
- WASD and touch-pad movement respond immediately.
- Punch scores 1 only during an active hit window.
- Side kick scores 2 only during an active hit window.
- Round kick scores 2 only during an active hit window.
- Blitz produces forward entry and scores 1 only on contact.
- Blocking prevents scoring and shows block feedback.
- Fighters do not occupy the same physical space.
- Hit feedback includes contact spark and camera impact response.
- Score resets both fighters to center.
- First to 5 ends the match and restart works.
- Android touch UI exposes punch, side kick, round kick, block, and blitz.
